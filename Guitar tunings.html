Guitar tunings

Pick a tuning to see each string’s note and how far it moves from standard. Click a string label to hear it. Click a fret to press that string there (one fret per string), then strum the chord.

Strings run from 6 (lowest) to 1 (highest). Blue values show how many semitones a string moves from standard. Octave numbers use scientific pitch, where standard low E is E2. Amber dots are pressed frets: click one again to release it, or pick a fret in the table. Pressed frets stay in place when you switch tunings.
